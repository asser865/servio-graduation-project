Deactivate Your Account?

Your account will be put on pause. Here's what that means:

  • Your profile will be hidden from technicians and search results.
  • Active bookings will be kept but no new ones can be made.

Permanently Delete Your Account?

This action is permanent and cannot be undone.

The following data will be permanently erased:

  • Your profile, photo, and all personal information
  • All booking history and receipts
  • Saved addresses and preferences
  • All reviews you've submitted
